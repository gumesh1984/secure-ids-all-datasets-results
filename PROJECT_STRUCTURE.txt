Secure IDS reproducibility package
==================================

secure_ids_pipeline.py
    Complete executable preprocessing, model-training, evaluation, privacy-
    attack, ablation, security-processing, and result-packaging pipeline.

experiment_config.py
    Compact record of the reported hyperparameters and random seeds.

requirements.txt
    Python package requirements.

README.md
    Dataset preparation and local/Google Colab reproduction instructions.

data/*.README.txt
    Dataset placeholders. Raw datasets are intentionally not redistributed.

results/
    Directly viewable principal tables and confusion-matrix figures.

reviewer_results_all_datasets.zip
    Complete results archive, including all per-dataset/per-seed reviewer
    reconstruction, membership-inference, attribute-inference, and ablation
    experiment tables.
